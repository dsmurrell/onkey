# Changelog

## 0.3.0 · 2026-11-02

OnKey in 19 languages, lyrics that keep time in Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, French and German, and starter songs in your language.

- Choose your language from the globe in the top bar, on the welcome screen or in Settings. Six have full support, lyrics included (Spanish for Latin America and Spain, Portuguese, Italian, French, German); twelve more are early. All are machine translations you can help improve.
- Lyrics in Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, French and German now light up word by word in time with the singer; songs in Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Hindi and other scripts show their lines whole.
- Songs to try in your language, and clicking a starter song now plays it in Spotify, as a Library card does.
- A song with no lyrics says so, and "Look again" asks LRCLIB straight away; when LRCLIB is down, OnKey tells you and the notes carry on.
- The Mac menu bar, system prompts and the website (singonkey.app) are in your language too.
- Fixes: switching songs in Spotify is cleaner, the What's new dot shows only after an update, the microphone is chosen from OnKey's own menu, Report a problem no longer shrinks its text box, and a song's range line is peach when it doesn't simply fit.

All the details: https://singonkey.app/changelog/

## 0.2.0 · 2026-10-31

Scoring levels, your vocal range, Pause listening, and audio interfaces that just work.

- Scoring levels in Settings: Relaxed (as before), Steady and Pro. Vibrato is judged on the note's centre.
- Your vocal range from your takes, in History, with how each song fits it and a filter in the Library.
- Mark a stretch "Not the melody" on a take, such as a backing vocal, and it's left out of the song's score.
- Pause listening (⌘⇧P, the Live chip or the OnKey menu): OnKey hears nothing until you resume.
- Your voice is heard on whichever input of an audio interface your mic is on; virtual devices are listed apart.
- Challenge a friend to beat your score, Fade the guide on songs you know, and Learn a song again.
- Fixes: Play in the Library starts the song, quitting is instant, lyrics line up better, listening back stays in
  step after a skip, a paused song shows when it'll be scored, and long radio streams stop at 20 minutes.

All the details: https://singonkey.app/changelog/

## 0.1.0 · 2026-10-22

The first public release.

- Learns the melody of any song playing in Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube in Chrome, the first time you play it,
  on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded.
- Live pitch line against the singer's notes, with synced lyrics, a score for every take and the best bits marked.
- Takes to hear back, with your voice over the notes, and a short video of a take to share (your voice, no music).
- Starter songs and playlist, a library of learned songs, and an in-app updater.
